## Research projects

### [EV Charging Scheduling with Deep Q-Network](https://github.com/thaothankfully/EV-Charging-Scheduling-DQN)

A Deep Q-Network framework for optimizing electric vehicle charging under stochastic electricity prices and driving demand.

- **Method:** Deep Reinforcement Learning, DQN
- **Application:** Smart EV charging scheduling
- **Objective:** Minimize charging costs for EV individual users while satisfying future driving demand

### [EV Charging Demand Forecasting](https://github.com/thaothankfully/EV-charging-demand-forecasting)

A comparative study of statistical, temporal, spatial, and spatio-temporal models for forecasting hourly EV charging-station occupancy.

- **Method:** ARIMA, LSTM, GCN, and GCN-LSTM
- **Application:** Urban EV charging demand forecasting and charging infrastructure planning
- **Objective:** Compare different forecasting approaches and evaluate the contribution of temporal and spatial dependencies.

### [Thao Dien Metro Station HCMC Shading - Walkability Optimization](https://github.com/roxannen9n/thao-dien-metro-station-shading-walkability-optimization/tree/main)

A spatial optimization study of tree-planting locations to improve shade along walking routes to Thao Dien metro station.

- **Method:** GIS + MILP
- **Application:** Pedestrian access to metro stations and urban tree-planting planning
- **Objective:** Select tree locations that maximize population-weighted reductions in sun exposure within a tree-count budget.
